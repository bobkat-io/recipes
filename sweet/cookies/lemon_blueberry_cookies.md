# Lemon Blueberry Cookies
> Adapted from [Serious Eats: Soft and Chewy Sugar Cookies](https://www.seriouseats.com/soft-and-chewy-sugar-cookie-recipe).

## Ingredients 
- 7-8 small lemons
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice or lemon syrup (see dough prep instructions)
- 3/4 cup dried / dehydrated blueberries (NOT freeze dried, but shriveled and chewy/raisin-like)
- 8 ounces unsalted butter (2 sticks; 225 g); firm but pliable, about 60°F (16°C)
- 10 ounces granulated sugar (1 1/2 cups; 280 g), plus another ~1/2 cup for rolling
- 2 1/2 teaspoons baking powder (10 g)
- 1 1/4 teaspoons Diamond Crystal kosher salt (5 g)
- 1 tablespoon vanilla bean paste
- 1 large egg, cold
- 11 1/4 ounces all-purpose flour (about 2 1/2 cups, spooned; 320 g)

## Directions
### Dough Prep
1. Do not preheat the oven, dough needs to chill
1. In a small prep bowl, zest 6 of the lemons over the 280g granulated sugar
1. In another small bowl, zest 1 lemon over the other 1/2 cup granulated sugar and set aside
1. Halve the lemons then juice the halves. Set aside 2 tbsp of lemon juice, OR use juice to prepare lemon syrup following https://www.seriouseats.com/fresh-lemon-syrup-recipe
1. Combine 8 oz butter, 280g granulated sugar with lemon zest, 10g baking powder, 5g kosher salt, and 1 tbsp vanilla bean paste in the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with a paddle attachment
1. Mix on low to moisten, increase to medium and beat until fluffy, pale ivory, and soft, about 5 minutes
1. Add 1 egg and 2 tbsp lemon juice or lemon syrup. Continue beating until smooth, about 2 minutes more, pausing to scrape down the sides of the bowl with a rubber spatula as needed
1. Resume mixing on low. Sprinkle in 320g all purpose flour, and continue mixing until a thick dough is formed. Do not overmix!
1. Using a spatula, mix in the 3/4 cup dried blueberries by hand
1. Cover and chill dough in the fridge until firm

### Baking
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and line cookie sheets with silpat
1. Scoop dough using a 2 tbsp cookie scoop (~26 cookies). Tumble each ball of dough in the lemon sugar so it is coated on all sides
1. Arrange cookies on prepared half sheet pan, leaving 2 1/2 inches of space in between cookies. Do not flatten; cookies will spread on their own
1. Bake until puffed and pale gold around the edges, but still steamy in the middle, about 15 minutes
1. Cool directly on sheet pan until firm, about 10 minutes
1. Enjoy warm, or allow to cool completely, then store in an airtight container for up to 2 days at room temperature
