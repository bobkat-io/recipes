# Lemon Blueberry Cookies
> Adapted from [Serious Eats: Soft and Chewy Sugar Cookies]([https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/packages/baking-guide/cookies-and-bars/50-chocolate-chip-cookies](https://www.seriouseats.com/soft-and-chewy-sugar-cookie-recipe)).

## Ingredients 
- 7-8 small lemons
- 1.5-2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 3/4 cup dried blueberries
- extra granulated sugar
- 8 ounces unsalted butter (2 sticks; 225 g); firm but pliable, about 60°F (16°C)
- 10 ounces granulated sugar (1 1/2 cups; 280 g), plus another ~1/2 cup for rolling
- 2 1/2 teaspoons baking powder (10 g)
- 1 1/4 teaspoons Diamond Crystal kosher salt (5 g)
- 1 tablespoon vanilla bean paste
- 1 large egg, cold
- 11 1/4 ounces all-purpose flour (about 2 1/2 cups, spooned; 320 g)

Round 2:
6 zest of lemon over sugar 
2 zest over sugar for rolling (only used like half of the cup of sugar for rolling though)

## Directions
1. Do not preheat the oven, dough needs to chill
1. In a small prep bowl, zest 6 of the lemons over the 280g granulated sugar
1. In another small bowl, zest 1 lemon over the other 1/2 cup granulated sugar and set aside
